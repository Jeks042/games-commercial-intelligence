# Player-feedback investigation brief

The immediate commercial decision is what to investigate before changing product messaging or acquisition priorities. The review evidence supports specific questions for product and support teams. It does not establish their frequency, financial impact or relative priority across titles. No spend change is recommended from these records alone.

The evidence below comes from the fixed two-cohort source and the fresh 110-record context check described in the [findings report](Stage%205%20player%20findings.md). References are audit indices in the [coded context notes](../data/review-refinement/audit/context-check-v1-20261008.json); each binds to the original project review key and text fingerprint. Full source text stays private. The examples were selected by diagnostic strata, not randomly for population inference. Statements about patches, roadmaps, hardware and defects are reviewer reports, not independently verified facts.

Across the portfolio, the useful distinction is between a reported experience, a reproducible product issue and an economically material problem. Those require different evidence. The current source has no support-ticket volumes, sales, refund-rate denominator, acquisition outcomes or validated aspect labels. Lifetime recommendation totals provide retrieval-current context only; there is no lifetime text corpus for theme comparisons. Historical sentiment and causal promotion/patch effects remain unavailable.

This brief is a reviewable investigation agenda. Final Stage 5 acceptance remains pending. The next commercial stage must carry these limitations forward into scenarios rather than turn diagnostic examples into measured demand or validated forecasts.
