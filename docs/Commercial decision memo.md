# Commercial portfolio decision

**Decision: monitor and investigate; no change to promotional spend or portfolio allocation is approved from this public evidence.** The accepted 6 October 2026 snapshot, bounded pricing records and qualified review examples support targeted questions. They do not establish paid-unit demand, contribution, returns or causal promotional response.

| Workstream | Evidence supporting investigation | Next decision input |
|---|---|---|
| Product and expectation diagnosis | WUCHANG and Assetto Corsa EVO have lifetime positive shares of 57.33% and 58.95%; audited examples raise distinct combat/readability and feature/performance questions. These are self-selected reports, not verified defects or current concern prevalence. | Current-build QA, support volumes, player research and verified product specifications. |
| Eiyuden response and offer context | The only qualifying direct group has three references. Eiyuden's lifetime positive share is 13.24 percentage points below their median, with a lower dated final price. Returned provider-container discounts give bounded context only. | Validate quality-of-life and expectation hypotheses; reconcile SKU, traffic, conversion, paid units and contribution before considering a promotion. |
| Catalogue observation | Assetto Corsa, Competizione and DEATH STRANDING show 13,017, 3,117 and 2,574 concurrent players at retrieval, with lifetime positive shares above 92%. One observation does not establish sustained activity or promotional efficiency. | Comparable weekly observations plus platform/country sales, contribution and campaign evidence. |

These workstreams are investigation paths, not a severity or return ranking. The [twelve-title investigation brief](Player%20feedback%20investigation%20brief.md) specifies a concrete question and validating evidence for every portfolio title; the report retains those bindings.

**Allocation gates:** Invest requires verified incremental returns and capacity; Promote requires a reconciled contribution case and governed response test; Maintain requires evidence about the actual current allocation and operating objectives; Deprioritise requires opportunity-cost and strategic evidence. Those inputs are unavailable. Monitor is therefore the supported action for all twelve titles, with different investigation paths and no assertion that their economics are equivalent.

**Pricing discipline:** a hypothetical 10% cut requires approximately 11.1% more paid units merely to preserve gross storefront transaction value, before costs, with exact thresholds affected by penny rounding. The scenario cases are declared assumptions; engagement never becomes purchases. They supply requirements to test, not expected outcomes or net returns.

**Evidence needed before money moves:** dated SKU/platform/country sales, publisher net receipts, fees/tax/refunds, contribution costs, campaign exposure and spend, controlled response evidence, and player/support validation. Forecasts and empirical forecast error remain unavailable. Historical SKU continuity, annual pricing, promotion causality, elasticity and ROI are not established. The Power BI project is a decision-support presentation of accepted evidence; native refresh, interaction and final report acceptance were verified separately in [Stage 7](Stage%207%20executive%20findings.md).
